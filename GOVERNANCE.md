# Governance

## Ownership and steward

EmissionGate is owned and stewarded by its maintainer, **Mohd Javed** ([@MohdJaved01](https://github.com/MohdJaved01)),
as a personal project. The maintainer confirmed this on 19 Nov 2026. It started as a hackathon submission
(`docs/hackathon/`) and is published under the Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`).

## Decisions

- Design decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`. An ADR marked *proposed* changes nothing until the
  maintainer accepts it. A changed decision gets a new ADR that supersedes the old one; history is not edited.
- The non-negotiable invariants in `AGENTS.md` change only through an accepted ADR.
- The maintainer approves every merge to `main`. `.github/CODEOWNERS` lists who reviews which paths.

## Contributions

Contributions are welcome through pull requests. Unless stated otherwise, a contribution is licensed
under Apache-2.0, as section 5 of the licence provides. Contributors keep their copyright.

## Security

Report vulnerabilities privately as described in `SECURITY.md`, never in a public issue.

## Changes to this document

The maintainer may revise this document. If the project gains more maintainers or moves to a company or
foundation, this file records the new steward and how decisions are made.
